Personal Statement

Jorge Morais — Beyond the Code

My journey into software development didn't begin in a classroom, it began with two decades of experience that taught me how people, systems and decisions interact under pressure. From four years in the Portuguese Army, where I led teams in operational contexts including international missions, to seven years managing teams at McDonald's, and then four years in real estate building client relationships from scratch, every chapter was an education in problem-solving, responsibility and human dynamics.

That experience shaped how I approach engineering: with structure, accountability, and a clear understanding that software exists to serve people. As Full-Stack Tech Lead at Balanças Marques, I bring both technical depth and the kind of leadership clarity that only comes from having led teams in genuinely demanding environments, long before writing a single line of code.

In parallel with my engineering career, I am currently completing my first book, Código Mental, a 150-page work in Portuguese on consciousness, personal growth and the kind of mental clarity that makes us better at whatever we do. It draws from real experiences across my career and life, and was written with a clear intention: to leave something lasting for my children, and to contribute something meaningful beyond my professional output. Writing a full book while working full-time as a developer and team lead has been its own exercise in discipline, focus and long-form thinking.

Professionally, my current frontier is the intersection of AI and Industrial IoT — building production systems where LLMs (RAG, MCP, agents) close the loop with real hardware and real telemetry. That is where I am investing seriously now, because it is where the next decade of software for the physical world is being written, and because it is the one combination my career has uniquely prepared me for.

I also keep a long-term curiosity for quantum computing and quantum physics — the probabilistic nature of quantum systems, the implications for cryptography and optimization, and the philosophical depth of quantum mechanics resonate strongly with how I think about complexity and uncertainty in systems design. It is a frontier I intend to engage with eventually, but I am intentionally not letting a more distant interest dilute the focus my current work demands.

This year I also took a significant step toward formalising my academic foundation by enrolling in the university entrance preparation programme at Universidade Aberta, with the goal of pursuing a degree in Computer Engineering. It is a conscious choice to invest in rigorous, formal education alongside an already active professional career, because I believe depth matters, and that the best developers combine hands-on experience with solid theoretical grounding.

I am not a developer who simply ships code and moves on. I think about the systems I build, the teams I work with, and the broader context in which technology operates. If you are looking for someone who brings both technical seniority and genuine human depth to the table, I would welcome the conversation.
