## 🎨 TITLE STRUCTURE PATTERN

All titles follow this SEO-optimized pattern:

```
[Primary Keyword] | Jorge Morais [Secondary Keyword]
```

**Examples:**
- `Projects & Portfolio | Jorge Morais Full Stack Dev`
- `About Jorge Morais | Senior Developer Experience & Bio`
- `Certifications & Education | Jorge Morais CS50 Harvard`

**Why this works:**
1. ✅ **Primary keyword first** (better SEO)
2. ✅ **Brand name prominent** (Jorge Morais)
3. ✅ **Secondary keyword for context** (Full Stack Dev, CS50, etc.)
4. ✅ **Pipe separator** (clean, modern look in SERPs)
5. ✅ **50-60 character sweet spot**

## 🎯 CHARACTER COUNT GUIDE

| Range | Assessment | Action |
|-------|------------|--------|
| < 30 | ❌ Too short | Expand with keywords |
